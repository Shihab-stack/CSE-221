Problem B: Calculator

For each test case, evaluate one operation written as "calculate a OP b".
The operands a and b are non-negative integers no greater than 1000. OP is one
of +, -, *, /, %, &, |, ^, <<, or >>. These are addition, subtraction,
multiplication, division, modulo, bitwise AND, bitwise OR, bitwise XOR, left
shift, and right shift. Division is real-number division; all other operations
are integer operations. Division and modulo inputs have a nonzero divisor.
Every result is within the signed 64-bit integer range.

Input
The first line contains T (1 <= T <= 1000). Each of the next T lines contains
"calculate a OP b".

Output
Print one result per test case. For division, an absolute or relative error up
to 10^-6 is accepted.

Example
Input:
5
calculate 1 + 3
calculate 9 / 4
calculate 9 % 4
calculate 5 << 1
calculate 5 >> 1
Output:
4
2.25
1
10
2

Forbidden keywords in the solution: eval, sort, open, file, creat(, fstream,
thread, process, system(, exec(.
